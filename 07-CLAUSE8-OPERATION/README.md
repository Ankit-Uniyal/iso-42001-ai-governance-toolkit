## 8.1 — Operational Planning and Control

The organisation must plan, implement, control and maintain the processes needed to meet AIMS requirements and to implement the actions determined in Clause 6.

Key requirements:
- Establish criteria for the processes and for the acceptance of AI systems
- Control the processes in accordance with those criteria
- Keep documented information sufficient to show the processes were carried out as planned
- Control planned changes, and review the consequences of unintended changes
- Control externally provided processes, products and services relevant to the AIMS

See OPERATIONAL-CONTROLS-REGISTER.md, AI-LIFECYCLE-MANAGEMENT-PROCEDURE.md, AI-DEPLOYMENT-CHECKLIST.md, AI-CHANGE-CONTROL-PROCEDURE.md, AI-MODEL-CARD-TEMPLATE.md and the three supplier documents.

## 8.2 — AI Risk Assessment

Perform AI risk assessments at planned intervals and when significant changes occur, in line with the process established under Clause 6.1.2. Records are held in `05-CLAUSE6-PLANNING/AI-RISK-REGISTER.md`.

## 8.3 — AI Risk Treatment

Implement the AI risk treatment plan established under Clause 6.1.3 and retain documented information on the results. See `05-CLAUSE6-PLANNING/RISK-TREATMENT-PLAN.md`.

## 8.4 — AI System Impact Assessment

Conduct impact assessments on individuals, groups and society, in line with the process established under Clause 6.1.4, and retain the results. See AI-SYSTEM-IMPACT-ASSESSMENT.md.

> **Note:** ISO/IEC 42001:2023 Clause 8 contains only 8.1 to 8.4. AI system life cycle requirements sit in Annex A domain A.6, and supplier and customer requirements sit in Annex A domain A.10, implemented here through the Clause 8.1 operational controls.
