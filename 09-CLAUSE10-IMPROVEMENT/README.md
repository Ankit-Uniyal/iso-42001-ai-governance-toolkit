## 10.1 — Continual Improvement

Continually improve the suitability, adequacy and effectiveness of the AIMS. Improvement inputs come from monitoring results, audit findings, incidents, nonconformities and management review outputs. See CONTINUAL-IMPROVEMENT-LOG.md.

## 10.2 — Nonconformity and Corrective Action

When a nonconformity occurs: react to it and deal with the consequences, evaluate the need for action to eliminate the root cause, implement the action, review its effectiveness, and update AIMS risks and controls if required. Retain documented information on the nature of the nonconformity and the results of corrective action. See NCR-REGISTER.md and AI-INCIDENT-RESPONSE-PROCEDURE.md.

> **Note on clause order:** In ISO/IEC 42001:2023, Clause 10.1 is *Continual improvement* and Clause 10.2 is *Nonconformity and corrective action*. This is the reverse of the order used in some other management system standards.
